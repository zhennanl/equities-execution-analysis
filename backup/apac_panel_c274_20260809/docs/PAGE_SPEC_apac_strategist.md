# PAGE SPEC — APAC Rebalance Panel

*c-231. Same contract as `PAGE_SPEC_review_db.md`: every
autonomous change to `views/apac_strategist.py` is checked
against this file. A change that cannot be justified from here
does not ship.*

---

## 1. READER

A CLSA PT trader who covers **more than one APAC market** and
has a review coming. He already knows what a rebalance is. He
wants three numbers before the meeting: how big, how violent,
and when does it move.

He is also the person most likely to be embarrassed by this
page in front of a client, which is why section 1 and section 8
exist.

## 2. THE JOB

**Show what 2,078 name-events across twelve markets say about
index-review execution — and be explicit that it is the
descriptive half of the Taiwan playbook, not the forecasting
half.**

Explicitly out of scope:

- prediction of any kind
- crowding, completion, squeeze risk (Tier 2 — Taiwan only)
- auction decomposition (Tier 3 — Taiwan 2023-05+)
- anything requiring a market-adjusted return series

## 3. THE PAGE COMPUTES NOTHING

Every figure is read from `data/index_strategist_qa.json`,
generated by `scripts/index_strategist_qa.py`. This is a hard
rule with a test behind it (`test_the_page_computes_nothing`):
the view may not import `statistics`, call `median(`, use
`np.` or `groupby`.

The reason is not purity. A view that does its own arithmetic
will eventually disagree with the document generated from the
same data, and the reader has no way to know which one is
wrong.

## 4. THE CAVEATS ARE THE PRODUCT

Three facts must be on screen, in the amber block, not as grey
text under a chart:

1. **Survivorship** — ten of twelve markets are priced from a
   survivors-only source; their deletion rows are biased.
   Taiwan and India keep the dead.
2. **Market adjustment, and the raw number kept** — every
   return is excess over that market's own local-currency
   benchmark, with the raw total return beside it.

   *This clause was reversed in c-274 and the reversal is
   part of the spec, not a silent edit.* It previously read
   "no market adjustment — returns are total, not excess",
   which was the honest label while no benchmark existed for
   every market. `apac_market_proxy.py` has since harvested
   one for all twelve at 98.5%+ coverage against the actual
   event days, so the old caveat was no longer describing the
   data. Taiwan routes to `twii_daily.json` rather than
   Yahoo's `^TWII`, which begins 2014-06 and would cover only
   79% of Taiwan's windows.

   **The raw column is not optional.** The size of the
   adjustment is itself the finding — on Taiwan additions it
   removes roughly 45% of the published drift, and in 15 of
   24 reversion cells the market's part carries the OPPOSITE
   sign to the excess, meaning the raw figure did not merely
   overstate the move, it pointed the wrong way. A page
   showing only the adjusted number would be more correct and
   less useful. Tested (`test_raw_returns_survive_beside_the
   _adjusted_ones`).
3. **Non-independence** — n counts name-events; names in one
   review share a market move.

Plus one per-market caveat: selecting a survivors-only market
in section 7 raises its own block.

A version of this page without these is more impressive and
less true. Tested.

## 5. SECTIONS

Numbered contiguously 1-9, in this order:

1. **What this panel can answer** — the three caveats and the
   scale of the sample.
2. **How big is the print** — T-multiple, median and p90.
3. **How many reviews are not events** — the quiet-event rate.
   Its own section because it is the planning fact that
   surprises people.
4. **Effective-day risk** — median and p90 absolute move.
5. **Announcement pop, or the drift** — where the move happens.
6. **Does the print revert** — excess return from the effective
   close forward, on a STRICT 20-session horizon. Added c-274.
   This section could not exist before the ±20 top-up: `rev20`
   was `min(effective + 20, last available)`, which does not
   fail on a short window, it returns a 15- or 18-session
   number and pools it with the twenties. The table must carry
   **n₂₀ beside n** so a market with windows too short to
   answer is visible rather than averaged in.
7. **Which schedule beat the close** — with the "unconditional,
   and ALL_DAY1 is not executable" caveat above the table.
8. **One market at a time** — the per-market brief.
9. **What this page cannot tell you** — the Tier 2/3 gap, and
   the pointer to the retrieval register.

Section 9 is not an afterthought. It is the section that keeps
the other eight honest, and it does not move to the bottom of
an expander.

## 6. FINDINGS ARE NOT ASSERTED HERE

The narrative read lives in `docs/INDEX_STRATEGIST_READ_APAC.md`
and is labelled judgement. This page presents data and method.

The standing rule holds: candidate findings go to
`docs/CANDIDATE_FINDINGS.md` for Bill to promote. An asserted
finding is a claim he would have to defend in his interview,
not me.

## 7. ACCEPTANCE CHECKS

`tests/test_apac_strategist_page.py`. Done when all pass:

  present

  "Taiwan only"
- the view imports no statistics machinery

## 8. NON-GOALS

- no prediction, probability or recommendation
- no positioning or crowding read until the data exists
- no new number computed in the view
- no removal of a caveat to make a chart cleaner
