## 1. Prediction & screening — the call sheet plus its uncertainty

Firm calls where the arithmetic is decisive; a probability-ranked
SHORTLIST where it isn't; a declared blind band where the universe
cannot see. History's contribution: base rates (TW Aug QIRs:
changes in 7/11 years, median ~2 names; P(any del)=50%, P(any
add)=46%), the cadence rule (79% of TW deletions happen at SAIRs —
an Aug-QIR delete needs a sub-floor story, not a migration story),
churn constraints (TW re-add-within-4 rate: 0% — kills the naive
"deleted names bounce back" trade), and the hazard framing
(boundary breaches convert at ~2/3 per SAIR; the residents
1101/1326/2207 are known by name).

## 2. Positioning & crowding surveillance — the daily read

TWT93U gives Taiwan daily per-name short/SBL balances — almost

Historical insight: crowding predicts PRINT CHARACTER, not just
risk — CONSENSUS names printed at/below last price despite 44-71%

## 3. Pre-positioning economics — should anyone lean early?

TW adds are momentum events (6/7 recent adds ran +30-107% in the

## 4. Capacity & liquidity pre-planning — the T-day cards

Per shortlist name, precompute what conversion implies: 1101 ->

window must start working almost immediately after announcement —
locates, borrow, and envelope conversations pre-staged this week.
