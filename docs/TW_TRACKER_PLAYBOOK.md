# The passive tracker's three questions

Generated by `scripts/tw_tracker_playbook.py`.

## The number a tracker asks first

Not days of ADV — the order does not go through the session, it goes through the close. So:

> **How many times this name's own normal closing auction is my order?**

An MSCI index mover puts about 79% of its effective-day volume through Taiwan's 13:25–13:30 call, against 9.5% on an ordinary day (IB 5-minute panel, Taiwan, n=43 index-mover windows). So an ordinary close absorbs roughly 0.095 × ADV shares, and the requirement in units of that is a plain multiple.

| | name | index demand | ADV days | × its normal close |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Winbond Electronics Corporation (2344) | 176.6m shares | 1.27 | **13.4×** |
| 2 | Nanya Technology Corporation (2408) | 82.3m shares | 1.02 | **10.8×** |
| 3 | Nan Ya Printed Circuit Board Corporation (8046) | 14.0m shares | 0.80 | **8.4×** |

Ten times the normal close is a different conversation from one times, and neither is visible in a days-of-ADV number.

## What this is not

- the closing-bar share is a MEDIAN across 43 index-mover windows and is applied to every name alike; a quiet name's own close may be thinner
- Taiwan's 5-second auction file is market-wide, so there is no per-name auction series to check this against
- the multiple is the SIZE OF THE ASK, not a slippage forecast — nothing here predicts price

## The three phases

### 1 · Now, before the announcement (to 12 Aug)

**Is the market already positioned for this?**

- *Metric* — 25-session excess return over TAIEX, and foreign net buying in days of ADV
- *Historical reference* — a typical Taiwanese addition arrives having risen +7.0% (positive 73% of the time) and having drawn +0.60 ADV days of foreign buying
- *Proposed threshold* — below -1.5% is the bottom quartile of the sample
- *Why it matters* — a name the market has already bought is a name whose index demand is partly met before the print

these four sit at the 2nd to 15th percentile — the anticipation that normally precedes an addition has not happened. That is the one genuinely unusual feature of this review and it cuts both ways: less pre-positioning to unwind, and less confirmation that the market agrees with the call.

### 2 · Announcement to effective (12–31 Aug)

**Is the flow arriving, and on schedule?**

- *Metric* — cumulative foreign net buying in ADV days, against the drift accrual path
- *Historical reference* — the median addition draws +0.53 ADV days over this leg and +1.04 cumulatively from 20 sessions before the announcement; half the drift has accrued by session 5 of about thirteen
- *Proposed threshold* — foreign buying under -0.09 ADV days by the halfway point is the bottom quartile
- *Why it matters* — the flow that has already arrived is flow that will not compete with you in the auction

the drift accrues close to linearly, so no date is special and there is nothing to wait for. What IS informative is the flow: if the shares are not moving, the demand is still ahead of you and the close carries it.

### 3 · The effective date (31 Aug)

**Did the close absorb it, and at what price?**

- *Metric* — effective-day volume as a multiple of ADV, and the close against the day's own VWAP
- *Historical reference* — the median addition prints 6.6x ADV (p90 13.6x), and across markets the close lands within a third of a percent of the day's VWAP
- *Proposed threshold* — a print under 2.9x ADV means the flow did not arrive; over 13.6x means it arrived with company
- *Why it matters* — for a tracker the effective close is not an execution choice, it is the benchmark; every alternative buys P&L with tracking error

the close is the benchmark, so executing in it is zero tracking error by definition. The measured dislocation is small — which is an argument FOR the close and against working the session.
