# Taiwan's closing auction — how much can the close absorb

Generated by `scripts/tw_auction_microstructure.py` into `data/tw_auction_microstructure.json`.

## Scope, before anything else

- **2,815 trading days**, 2015-01-05 to 2026-08-05, from TWSE's own 5-second disclosure.
- **Market-wide.** MARKET-WIDE. There is no per-stock breakdown in this file, so every figure sizes the VENUE and none of them sizes a name.
- Columns were identified by behaviour, not by a header: matched quantity and value both freeze through order collection and jump at 13:30, and their ratio implies an average traded price of 34 TWD. Four other columns could not be identified and are unused.

## The auction is small, and that is the point

| | p25 | median | p90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| share of the day, by quantity | 3.12% | 3.63% | 5.43% |
| share of the day, by value | 5.70% | 6.60% | 9.56% |

The median Taiwanese close prints 6.60% of the day's value — about USD 412m of turnover across the entire market. On a quiet day (p10) it is USD 170m.

Set that against the IB panel's per-name result: an MSCI index mover puts roughly **79%** of its effective-day volume through that same five minutes. The venue is thin; the index trade is not. That gap is the capacity problem in one line.

## Index days trade a different venue

| day type | n | median auction share of value |
|---|---|---|
| MSCI effective date | 30 | 30.32% |
| month-end, not an MSCI date | 114 | 11.22% |
| neither | 2,671 | 6.52% |

**THE CONTROL MATTERS AND IT WAS NEARLY SKIPPED.** 26/30 MSCI effective dates in this sample are month-ends, and month-end carries benchmark flow from every index family at once. Without the middle row a month-end effect would have been reported as an MSCI effect. It is not: month-end alone lifts the close from 6.52% to 11.22%, and the MSCI review lifts it again to 30.32% (p=0.0000).

**The close is deepest exactly when you need it.** Roughly five times its ordinary share. A capacity estimate built off a normal day understates the effective-date close by a factor of about 5, which is the difference between crossing at the close and working the session.

## The review type is a capacity input

MSCI runs a semi-annual review in May and November and a smaller quarterly one in February and August. The close knows the difference: 31.36% of the day's value on the 21 May/November dates against 19.34% on the 9 February/August ones (p=0.0005).

August 2026 is a QUARTERLY review, so the base case for its close is the lower number — around 19.34% of the day rather than the 31.36% a May or November print would bring. A desk has months of notice on this input.

## What this file cannot do

- **No per-stock split.** Sizing a single name's share of its own close needs the IB panel or TWSE per-stock intraday, neither of which reaches 2015.
- **Four unidentified columns.** An order-imbalance series would be the most useful thing here and the least defensible; it is not built.
- **Market-wide value is not a limit order book.** Knowing the close trades USD 412m does not tell you the depth available at a price.
