# Has anyone already bought the August additions?

Generated by `scripts/tw_prepositioning.py`. Flow data runs to **2026-08-05**; MSCI announces 2026-08-12, so the last 5 sessions before the announcement are unobserved.

## The answer

**NO EVIDENCE OF IT, and the cross-section is what makes that a finding rather than a market observation.**

3 of 3 carried candidates sat below the peer median for foreign net buying over the 20 sessions to 20260805 — while foreigners were NET BUYERS of the peer set over the same sessions.

| name | foreign net | percentile of peers | domestic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nanya Technology Corporation (2408) | -0.23 ADV days | 43% | +0.16 |
| Nan Ya Printed Circuit Board Corporation (8046) | -0.95 ADV days | 29% | +0.85 |
| Winbond Electronics Corporation (2344) | -0.02 ADV days | 45% | -0.18 |

The peer median was +0.09 ADV days over the same sessions, and foreigners bought the peer set by +1,875m shares in aggregate. **The absence is specific to these names, not to the market.**

For contrast, a typical Taiwanese addition draws +0.60 ADV days of foreign buying in the 20 sessions before its announcement, and 46% of its whole accumulation is done before MSCI speaks.

## Why this is suggestive and not conclusive

- foreign net is a NET — a fund building a position against a seller of the same size is invisible
- T86 separates residency, not mandate: a tracker, a long-only and a hedge fund are one row
- it measures flow, not holdings, so a position built in May and held since leaves no trace in a 20-day window
- the 130-name peer set is large-cap TWSE, not the whole market

## What cannot be measured here

- **Phison (8299)** — TPEx-listed and absent from T86, which is TWSE-only. It is also the name not carried, so the three measured are the three called.
- **8299** — Phison Electronics Corp.: no ADV or no T86 coverage
