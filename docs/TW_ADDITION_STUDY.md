# MSCI Taiwan additions — the historical study

Generated by `scripts/tw_addition_study.py` into `data/tw_addition_study.json`. Every figure below is interpolated from that file.

## The sample

- **124 windows** — 52 additions, 72 deletions — May15 to May26.
- 44 windows dropped for an estimated announcement date, 0 for a short window.
- Returns are excess over TAIEX, close to close. Day 0 is registry-dated announcements only. The deletion sample is survivor-safe — TWSE/TPEx day files retain delisted companies.

## The four legs of an addition

| | additions | deletions |
|---|---|---|
| pre-announcement drift (median) | +6.96% | -5.04% |
| announcement gap | +1.10% | -1.07% |
| drift, ann+1 to eff-1 | +2.21% | -2.34% |
| effective-day move | +0.54% | -0.96% |
| +5 sessions after | -4.05% | +0.71% |
| +20 sessions after | -5.29% | -2.18% |
| effective-day print, x ADV | 6.6x | 21.2x |
| ...p90 | 13.6x | 39.1x |

**The round trip is a coin flip, not a loss.** The median addition earns +2.21% to the effective date and gives back -5.29% over the next twenty sessions — but medians do not add. Summed event by event the round trip is +0.03% at the median, 50% of events negative, quartiles -9.48% to +10.01%. Its mean drift (+6.38%) is nearly three times its median, so the average is carried by a handful of events and a book sized on it is sized on those.

**The reversion is a pre-2023 result and the split is not significant either way.**

| | pre-2023 | post-2023 | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| drift (n=34 / 18) | +1.71% | +6.74% | 0.28 |
| +20 after (n=34 / 18) | -5.65% | +0.01% | 0.14 |
| print, x ADV (n=34 / 18) | 7.1x | 4.8x | 0.15 |

Reversion ran -5.65% with 76% of events negative before Feb-2023 and +0.01% with 50% since. At p=0.14 on n=18 the sample cannot establish that the effect has gone — only that it cannot establish it is still there. Per §0.3.6, a period split is not a controlled experiment: Taiwan's ETF complex and passive book changed over the same span and are not separated here.

**Most of the move precedes the news.** Pre-announcement drift is +6.96% at the median and positive 73% of the time — about three times what is left afterwards.

## Who is on the other side

Foreign net flow, in days of the name's own ADV, from TWSE T86 day files (n=42 additions, 65 deletions):

| leg | additions | deletions |
|---|---|---|
| 20 sessions before the announcement | +0.60 | -1.79 |
| announcement to effective | +0.53 | -1.51 |
| the effective day | +0.16 | -0.58 |
| the ten sessions after | -0.10 | -2.94 |
| cumulative to the print | +1.04 | -5.13 |

**Foreigners are the deletion and barely the addition.** A deletion draws 5.1 days of ADV of foreign selling to the print and another 2.9 after it; an addition draws 1.04 days of buying. Domestic institutions are within ±0.05 ADV days on both sides.

**The print is not ownership transfer.** On the effective day, foreign plus domestic net is 4.2% of the volume that printed on an addition and 7.3% on a deletion. That is the mechanism behind the reversion: there is no new holder to defend the price.

**Half the buying is done before the announcement** — 46% of the whole accumulation, at the median.

## Execution

| schedule | median saved | hit rate | tracking error |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100% at the effective close | +0.00% | 0% | 0.00% |
| 25% x last four sessions | +0.98% | 71% | 2.34% |
| 100% at announcement +1 | +4.96% | 60% | 10.83% |

The effective-close row is zero on both columns by construction — it is the tracker's benchmark, and it is the control that the other two are measured right. Half the drift has accrued by session 5 of about thirteen and 80% by session 12, so the curve is close to linear and no date is special.

## The negative results

**Nothing predicts the direction out of sample.** 6 rules fitted on 34 pre-2023 additions, scored on 18 since. NO RULE SURVIVES. The best is gap1 at lift +0.30, but it selects only 7 of 18 test events and its binomial p is 0.108 — against a 0.008 threshold once the 6 rules tried are accounted for. The addition drift is not predictable out of sample from anything measured here.

**106 hypotheses were tested**; the Bonferroni threshold is p<0.00047 and 6 survive: DEL.mid_flow_vs_print, asymmetry.print_size_p, borrow_build->vol_mult_eff, prevol->vol_mult_eff, prevol->vol_mult_eff, prior_moves->vol_mult_eff. Every one of them is about the SIZE of the print. None is about its direction.

**Five driver pairs were excluded for overlapping windows**, including the announcement gap against total alpha, which ranked first at rho +0.56 before it was removed and is arithmetic rather than evidence.

**Crowded reviews and repeat movers do nothing measurable.** Three-or-more-addition reviews versus solo ones, p=1.00; repeat movers versus first-timers, p=0.43.
