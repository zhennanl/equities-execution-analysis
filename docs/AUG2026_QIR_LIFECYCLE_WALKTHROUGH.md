## Now → Aug 10 — the prediction endgame (winning the trade)

The sales trader's last marketing push happens BEFORE anyone knows
the answers; broker selection is largely decided in this week. Desk
work:
- Finalize predicted adds/deletes (project protocol: refresh caps,
  resolve TW boundary names, calls locked before Aug-12).
- Send prediction packs with PRIOR GRADES attached (May-2026: TW
  17/17 adds; misses listed — the credibility artifact).
- Envelope conversations while terms are still negotiable, armed
  with the decade cost tables (which side of this event class
  rewards discretion).
- Crowding watch: short builds into likely deletes — tag each name
  CONSENSUS vs UNPRICED.
- Market-access prep: a likely add that needs TW foreign-investor
  ID / China Connect eligibility / India FPI means the CLIENT must
  start paperwork now, not after announcement.

## Aug 11-12 — announcement: the single most important morning

Asia wakes Wednesday Aug-12 to the official list. First hours:
1. **Grade our own calls immediately and honestly** — hits AND
   misses go to clients same-morning. Nothing markets the desk
   better than a graded scorecard delivered before they ask.
2. Replace estimated flows with EXACT ones from official inclusion
   factors and index files.
3. Field the inquiry wave — orders and RFQs are won or lost in the
   ~48 hours after announcement, largely on what was sent before it.
Project-specific: Aug-12 grades the live Aug pack, and begins
arbitrating the demoted May-2026 "MSCI-add pop-decay" hypothesis
(decade study says CN adds grind up; this event is the referee).

## Aug 12-14 — terms and intake

- Risk-bid RFQs on blind profiles (minutes-level response windows).
- Envelope negotiation per event class; term sheets drafted with
  expected TD gain vs quadrature TE cost.
- Files arrive (FIX / Excel / IB chat) → normalize → compliance
  pre-flight → pre-trade pack → acknowledgment loop.
- **The critical calculation: the start schedule.** Any name
  needing 6+ ADV-days at a 25% participation cap must START ~Aug
  17-18 or it is flagged LATE START. Missing this flag now is the
  classic silent error that surfaces as an unfillable residual on
  Aug-28.

## Aug 17-27 — the window (the long middle)

- Work multi-day illiquids inside their envelopes.
- Daily monitoring loop: short-build deltas, foreign flows, A+3
  momentum reads feeding the per-name discretion decisions.
- Watch for list revisions and corporate actions touching names.
- Cross-client NETTING scan as orders accumulate (crossable flow =
  desk P&L and client price improvement).
- Client cadence: brief window updates — presence, not noise.
- Sub-deadlines: locates confirmed ~T-5 (Aug-24); FX arranged T-2
  (Aug-27).

## Friday Aug 28 — T-1, the staging night (second-most-crucial day)

- Final files reconciled LINE BY LINE against the official index
  file (shares, corp-action adjustments, late revisions).
- Auction orders staged per market with cutoff times.
- Run-sheet written: the follow-the-sun cascade, per-market cutoffs,
  and contingencies — which names carry limit-band lock risk.
  (Lesson from the 6919/2344 case studies: MID-WINDOW locks are the
  dangerous kind; PRINT-DAY locks usually favor the obligated flow —
  the band caps the price in the passive side's favor.)
- Completion plans for anything behind schedule.
Errors caught Sep-1 were almost always makeable-visible on Aug-28.

## Monday Aug 31 — T-day: the print

Follow-the-sun across Asia, each market a repetition of the same
close sequence:
1. **Taiwan first** — 13:30 close; indicative price/volume broadcast
   13:25-13:30 is THE real-time decision window: compare indicative
   volume to the expected T-multiple (THIN → the print will be
   violent, consider retreating envelope usage; RICH → the crowd is
   providing liquidity, size accordingly).
2. Japan 15:30, then Hong Kong (16:08-16:10 random close), SE Asia,
   India last.
3. Per market: MOC orders in before cutoff → indicative read → final
   envelope sizing → print → IMMEDIATE post-close snapshot (fills,
   auction share, gap vs last continuous price) while the next
   market is still open.
The day is mechanical certainty punctuated by a handful of judgment
moments — that is the design, not a disappointment. The desk's value
on T-day is flawless plumbing at scale plus the few discretionary
calls done well.

## Sep 1-2 — grade and learn

- TCA vs pre-trade estimates per basket; client debriefs out
  SAME-DAY (the artifact that wins the November SAIR).
- Completion legs for locked/unfilled residuals.
- Reversal-capture decisions on names expected to give back the pop
  (~50% reversal by T+5 measured on past events).
- Discretion decisions graded as counterfactuals; priors updated;
  the event enters the library.
- Project: full prediction grading after Sep-1; the Aug event's
  window data joins the decade panel.

## Crucial days, ranked

1. **Aug 11/12 — announcement.** Everything before it is
   preparation; everything after is consequence.
2. **Aug 31 — the print.** Where the benchmark lives.
3. **Aug 28 — T-1 staging.** Errors here are unrecoverable by T.
4. **~Aug 17 — start-schedule deadline** for 6+ ADV-day names.
5. **Sep 1 — the debrief** that markets the next event.
