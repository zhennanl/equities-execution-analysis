# CLSA — Agency-Only PT Desk: Where Differentiation Actually Lives

*Saved 2026-08-05 for interview reference. Full derivation and
sources: INDEX_REVIEW_EXPLAINED_QA.md Q31–Q33; related case

## The verified premise

CLSA positions itself as Asia's largest AGENCY-ONLY equity
brokerage — "unconflicted since 1986": no proprietary trading, no
market making, no banking conflicts; program trading, electronic
and high-touch execution are pure agency (clsa.com institutional
equities pages; industry press on the unconflicted model). Nuance
for completeness: parent CITIC Securities runs principal
businesses at group level, and facilitation policies can vary by
market — but agency-only is the verified CLSA model and brand.

## What agency-only genuinely removes

The risk-transfer axis: no guaranteed-close pricing, no capital
commitment, no principal netting. That business sits with
bulge-bracket desks. At the pure fill level, an agency
market-on-close order from CLSA and from any other desk receives
the same closing price.

## The four axes that survive — and one is STRENGTHENED

1. **Agency crossing.** Matching one client's tracker buy against
   another client's arbitrage unwind at the close needs no
   capital, and cross rates differ hugely by desk — pure client
   price-improvement, pure agency business.
2. **Advice and research — the axis where mandates are actually
   won.** Which changes are coming, what the close will look
   like, whether a flexible client should deviate from
   market-on-close (the ~590bps May-2026 demonstration: the
   scenario-conditioned split beat the close on the OVERCROWDED
   names). The basket is awarded BEFORE effective day on exactly
   this — and research-led mandate-winning is CLSA's actual
   historic franchise.
3. **Operational auction excellence.** Squeeze and limit
   handling, residual management, entry timing, reading the
   indicative feed — measured in TCA basis points even on
   "identical" mandates. (The Compal case: the tell — a
   10%-of-shares borrow balance — sat in free daily data; desks
   that read it advised differently.)
4. **The unconflicted position is ITSELF the differentiator for
   this product.** Index-rebalance flow is the most predictable,
   most front-runnable flow in equities, and leakage-sensitive
   clients route it to agency-only desks PRECISELY BECAUSE no
   prop book sits behind the wall. For rebalance baskets, no-prop
   is not a limitation — it is the pitch.

## The business conclusion (the interview thesis)

An agency-only desk cannot monetize risk pricing, so it must
monetize INFORMATION, OPERATIONS, and TRUST — advice quality
upfront, execution craft at the close, and proof (TCA)
afterward. That is precisely what the four-step analytics
platform is: Step 1 wins the mandate, Step 2 prices and explains
the close, Step 3 executes the auction and its failure modes,
Step 4 proves the value. The platform is not adjacent to CLSA's
model — it is aimed at the only axes on which an agency desk
competes.
